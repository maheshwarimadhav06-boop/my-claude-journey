# Day 11/60 – ATS Resume Optimizer & Resume Generator 📄✨

Today I built an **ATS Resume Optimizer & Resume Generator** using Claude AI.

## What I Learned

✅ How ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) scan resumes
✅ The importance of keywords and proper formatting
✅ How AI can transform a basic resume into an ATS-friendly document
✅ Generating professional resumes tailored to specific job roles

### Key Takeaway

A great resume isn't just about looking good—it's about being readable by both recruiters and ATS software. AI can significantly improve resume quality, keyword optimization, and overall structure.

This project helped me understand how technology can simplify the job application process and increase the chances of getting shortlisted.
