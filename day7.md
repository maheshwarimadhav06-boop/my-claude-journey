# 🚀 Day 7/60 – Claude Model Selection & Reasoning Effort

## 🎯 Topic

**Choose the right model before writing the perfect prompt**

## 💡 What I Learned

Today, I explored how selecting the right Claude model and reasoning effort can significantly impact the quality, speed, and depth of AI responses.

### Key Insight

A great prompt alone isn't enough. The model you choose determines how well the AI can reason, analyze, and solve complex problems.

### Biggest Learning

* Use faster models for simple tasks and quick iterations.
* Use advanced reasoning models for coding, problem-solving, research, and detailed analysis.
* Adjust reasoning effort based on task complexity instead of using maximum reasoning every time.
* The best results come from balancing **model capability, reasoning effort, and prompt quality**.

## 🔍 Observation

The same prompt can produce very different outputs depending on the selected model and reasoning level. Choosing the right model first often improves results more than repeatedly tweaking the prompt.

## 🚀 Takeaway

**Prompt Engineering is important, but Model Selection is the foundation.**
The right model + the right reasoning effort = better AI outcomes.
