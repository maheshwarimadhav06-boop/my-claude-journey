# 🚀 Day 3/60 – Role-Based Prompting with Claude AI

## 🎯 What I Learned

Today, I explored **Role-Based Prompting** — a technique where you assign a specific role to Claude before asking a question.

Instead of:
❌ "How do I improve my resume?"

Try:
✅ "Act as a senior technical recruiter and review my resume."

## 💡 Key Insight

The quality of AI responses improves significantly when the AI clearly understands **who it should be** and **what perspective it should use**.

## 🔥 Examples

- Software Engineer 👨‍💻
- Technical Recruiter 🎯
- Product Manager 📊
- Career Coach 🚀
- Marketing Expert 📈

## 📌 Takeaway

**Better role = Better context = Better output.**

Small prompting changes can lead to more accurate, practical, and professional responses.
