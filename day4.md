# 🚀 Day 4/60 – Chain-of-Thought Prompting with Claude AI

## 🎯 What I Learned

Today, I explored **Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting**, a technique that encourages AI to break down complex problems into smaller logical steps before generating an answer.

Instead of jumping directly to a solution, Claude follows a structured reasoning process, which often leads to more accurate and reliable results.

## 📝 Basic Prompt

Solve this problem.

## ✨ Chain-of-Thought Prompt

Think step by step, explain your reasoning, and then provide the final answer.

## 🔍 Key Observation

- Better logical reasoning
- Improved problem-solving accuracy
- More transparent decision-making
- Reduced chances of missing important details

## 💡 Takeaway

When tasks involve reasoning, analysis, calculations, or decision-making, asking Claude to think through the problem step by step can significantly improve the quality of the response.
